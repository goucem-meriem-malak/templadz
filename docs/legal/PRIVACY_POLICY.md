## English
- **Where data lives.** Templates, documents and settings are stored only on your computer (the app's user-data folder). The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and makes no network requests.
- **What we store locally.** Your templates, your language/theme settings and your activation code.
- **Personal data inside your documents.** Names, dates and IDs you type are processed only on your device to create your files. You decide where to save or send them.
- **Sharing.** "Share by email" saves a `.tpz` file and opens your email program; you attach and send it yourself. The email provider then handles it under its own policy.
- **Your control.** Delete templates in the app, or delete the user-data folder / uninstall the app to remove everything.
- **Payments and activation codes** are handled privately by the seller, outside the app.

- **Ce qui est stocké localement.** Vos modèles, vos réglages de langue/thème et votre code d'activation.

- **Votre contrôle.** Supprimez les modèles dans l'application, ou supprimez le dossier de données / désinstallez l'application.
- **Paiements et codes d'activation** : gérés en privé par le vendeur, hors de l'application.
